Private ESPN league? Bring it in with one click
  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Send to Trade Desk
  2. On fantasy.espn.com, open your league (any page of it) while logged in, then click that bookmark.
  3. Trade Desk opens with your league loaded. Click the bookmark again any time to refresh rosters.

  2. iPhone (Safari): bookmark any page (Share, then Add Bookmark). Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, open the new bookmark, paste the code over its address, and name it Send to Trade Desk.
    Android (Chrome): bookmark any page, then edit it the same way.
  3. Open your league on fantasy.espn.com in the same browser (logged in on the website, not the ESPN app).
    iPhone: open Bookmarks and tap Send to Trade Desk.
    Android: type Send to Trade Desk in the address bar and tap the bookmark in the suggestions.
  4. Trade Desk opens with your league loaded.

Your ESPN login never leaves ESPN: the bookmark reads your league on ESPN's own site and hands Trade Desk just the rosters and settings.
